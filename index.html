Features

Everything you need to run your business.

Stortrics gives you the tools that large chains use — right on your desktop, tablet, or phone. Simple enough for any staff member, powerful enough for your growing business.

All-in-One Checkout

Sell products, book services, or mix both in a single sale — one cart, one receipt, one system. Built for speed in busy shops.

  • Barcode scanning — point at the product and it adds to the bill instantly
  • Product search — type a name and find it in seconds; recent items appear first
  • Easy cart — add/remove items, adjust quantities, see total in real-time
  • Multiple payments — cash, bank transfer, POS card, USSD, split payment
  • Receipt printing — Bluetooth thermal printer or share via WhatsApp
  • Works on desktop, tablet, and phone — your choice

Inventory Management

Complete stock control for hundreds of products — from drugs in a pharmacy to gadgets in an electronics shop.

  • Product catalog — add items with barcodes, images, cost price, and selling price
  • Automatic stock updates — every sale deducts stock instantly, no manual counting
  • Low-stock alerts — get notified before you run out of fast-moving items
  • Stock adjustments — record deliveries, damages, or corrections with full audit trail
  • Categories — organize by any structure that fits your business

Sales Reports & Analytics

Know how your business is doing — anytime, anywhere. Daily reports delivered to your inbox.

  • Daily sales summary — today's revenue, transaction count, top products
  • Profit analysis — see exactly how much profit each product or service gives you
  • Top sellers — know which items move fastest so you can stock accordingly
  • Date range reports — compare this week to last week, this month to last month
  • Export to PDF — share reports via WhatsApp with partners or accountants
  • Email reports — daily revenue, profit, and top performers sent to your inbox automatically

Staff Management & Security

Protect your business with bank-level security and full accountability — know exactly who did what, and when.

  • Multiple staff accounts — each team member gets their own login
  • Role-based permissions — staff can sell, managers can adjust stock, only you see everything
  • PIN login — secure, easy, fast
  • Permanent audit trail — every action recorded (who, what, when) — cannot be deleted
  • Smart price protection — prevents selling below cost price, even by mistake

Real Story

A day at Oga Emeka's Gadget World.

See how a busy tech gadget store at Computer Village uses Stortrics to protect stock, catch fraud, and know exactly where every naira goes.

Oga Emeka runs a busy gadget store with 3 sales staff and a manager.

He stocks everything from Tecno and Infinix smartphones to HP laptops, Oraimo power banks, FreePods earbuds, and hundreds of accessories. Before Stortrics, he used exercise books and relied on trust — until he noticed phones going missing and profits not matching sales.

8:00 AM

Morning stock check — zero counting

Opens Stortrics on his laptop. Sees power banks are running low (5 left), calls his supplier before the weekend rush. No more 30-minute morning counts — the app already knows what's in stock from yesterday's sales.

10:30 AM

Peak hours — fast checkout

Customer buys an Infinix Note 40 (₦195,000) and Oraimo FreePods (₦12,000). Barcode scan, bank transfer reference, receipt printed. Total time: 40 seconds. Stock updates automatically — no exercise book needed.

1:00 PM

Catching fraud red-handed

Notices a stock count mismatch. Opens the audit log: a sales staff member sold a power bank yesterday, then processed a fake "return" 7 minutes later to pocket the cash. With exercise books, this scheme would be invisible.

💡 This scheme was costing ₦150,000/month. The permanent audit trail caught it immediately.

3:30 PM

Bulk order — protected margins

A businessman wants 5 laptops (₦380K each). Asks for a discount. The staff can't give one — the app won't allow it for their role. Oga Emeka logs in, adjusts to ₦360K/unit (still ₦40K profit each), approves. The audit log records every price change.

8:00 PM

End-of-day clarity

Today's summary: ₦2,850,000 in sales across 47 transactions. Total profit: ₦485,000. He taps "Export PDF" and WhatsApps it to his brother who handles the books. No staying late to count cash and tally receipts.

💡 "We did ₦2.85M today, ₦485K profit." Real numbers, not guesswork.

10:00 PM

Automatic backup at home

At home, Stortrics silently backs up the day's data over WiFi: 47 transactions, 15 stock adjustments, 2 price overrides. Everything is backed up. Nothing is lost.
