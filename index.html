Features

Everything you need to run your shop.

Stortrics gives you the tools that big supermarkets use — right on your phone, tablet, or laptop. Simple enough for your sales boys, powerful enough for your growing business.

Point of Sale

Built for speed in busy markets — process sales in 40 seconds flat.

  • Barcode scanning — point your phone at the box and it adds to the bill instantly
  • Product search — type "Tecno Camon" or "Oraimo" and find it in seconds
  • Easy cart — add/remove items, adjust quantities, see total in real-time
  • Multiple payments — cash, bank transfer, POS card, USSD, split payment
  • Receipt printing — Bluetooth thermal printer or share via WhatsApp
  • Mix services and products in a single sale — one cart, one receipt

Inventory Management

Complete stock control for hundreds of products and accessories.

  • Product catalog — add items with barcodes, images, cost price, and selling price
  • Automatic stock updates — every sale deducts stock instantly, no manual counting
  • Low-stock alerts — get notified when power banks or fast chargers run low
  • Stock adjustments — record deliveries, damages, or corrections with full audit trail
  • Categories — organize by Phones, Laptops, Power Banks, Earbuds, Accessories, Services

Sales Reports & Analytics

Know how your business is doing — anytime, anywhere.

  • Daily sales summary — today's revenue, transaction count, top products
  • Profit analysis — see exactly how much profit each item gives you
  • Top sellers — know which products move fastest
  • Date range reports — compare this week to last week, this month to last month
  • Export to PDF — share reports via WhatsApp
  • Email reports — daily revenue, profit, and top performers sent to your inbox

Real Story

A day at Oga Emeka's Gadget World.

See how a busy tech gadget store at Computer Village, Enugu uses Stortrics to protect stock, catch fraud, and know exactly where every naira goes.

Oga Emeka runs a busy gadget store with 3 sales boys and a manager.

He stocks everything from Tecno Camon 30 and Infinix Note 40 smartphones to HP laptops, Oraimo power banks, FreePods earbuds, and hundreds of accessories. Before Stortrics, he used exercise books and relied on trust — until he noticed phones going missing and profits not matching sales.

8:00 AM

Morning stock check — zero counting

Opens Stortrics on his tablet. Sees Oraimo power banks are running low (5 left), calls his Lagos supplier before the weekend rush. No more 30-minute morning counts — the app already knows what's in stock from yesterday's sales.

10:30 AM

Peak hours — fast checkout

Customer buys an Infinix Note 40 (₦195,000) and Oraimo FreePods (₦12,000). Barcode scan, bank transfer reference, receipt printed. Total time: 40 seconds. Stock updates automatically — Infinix drops from 12 to 11 units.

1:00 PM

Catching fraud red-handed

Notices a power bank count mismatch — 5 in the system, 4 on the shelf. Opens the audit log: his sales boy sold one at 4:45 PM yesterday, then processed a fake "return" 7 minutes later to pocket the cash. With exercise books, this scheme would be invisible.

💡 This scheme was costing ₦150,000/month. The permanent audit trail caught it immediately.

3:30 PM

Bulk order — protected margins

A businessman wants 5 Lenovo laptops (₦380K each). Asks for a discount. The sales boy can't give one — the app won't allow it for his role. Oga Emeka logs in, adjusts to ₦360K/unit (still ₦40K profit each), approves. The audit log records every price change.

8:00 PM

End-of-day clarity

Today's summary: ₦2,850,000 in sales across 47 transactions. Total profit: ₦485,000. He taps "Export PDF" and WhatsApps it to his brother who handles the books. No staying late to count cash and tally receipts.

💡 "We did ₦2.85M today, ₦485K profit." Real numbers, not guesswork.

10:00 PM

Automatic backup at home

At home, Stortrics silently backs up the day's data over WiFi: 47 transactions, 15 stock adjustments, 2 price overrides. He checks tomorrow's alerts — Oraimo FreePods at 15 units, reorder by Thursday. Everything is backed up. Nothing is lost.
